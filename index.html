How do I enquire or place an order?

Open a style, choose your preferred size and add it to your enquiry bag. Send the bag through WhatsApp to discuss available items and prices. An enquiry is not an order; confirm the final item, amount and payment arrangements directly.

How do I find the right size?

Use our size guide to prepare your measurements. Sizing varies by garment and supplier, so ask for the actual item’s measurements before ordering.

What are the delivery options?

Share your town or delivery destination when you enquire. Availability, delivery fees, dispatch timing and any collection arrangements must be agreed directly before payment. No fixed delivery promise is listed yet.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Please ask about the item’s return or exchange eligibility, time limits, condition requirements and any delivery charges before confirming an order. A final store policy has not yet been published.

Are the photos and prices current stock?

This launch collection is an inspiration edit with illustrative photography. Stock, exact designs, sizes, colours and prices are confirmed directly through WhatsApp. Online payment is not enabled.

What happens to my information?

Your saved styles and enquiry bag stay in this browser’s local storage. We do not collect form submissions or payment details on this site. If you send an enquiry, the information you choose to share is handled through WhatsApp.
